CallCenter AI

06

Thesis · UCLV

IoT control for business premises

Four zones of a business that watch themselves and save energy.

Warehouse, office, reception and sales each run on an ESP32 with temperature, gas, flame, water, light and motion sensors, plus a camera for surveillance. Everything reports over MQTT to Home Assistant, which drives lights and climate by rules and sends critical alerts and remote control through a Telegram bot.

My part. Architecture, firmware for each zone, wiring diagrams, Home Assistant and Node-RED automations, and the device budget.
